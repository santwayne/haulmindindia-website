How it works

Five steps.
Zero double entry.

Each step picks up exactly where the last one ended. Enter information once and it flows through dispatch, delivery, billing and accounts on its own.

Book

Create the LR, calculate freight and print the bilty.

Dispatch

Load LRs on a challan, assign vehicle and driver.

Unload

Record received, short and damaged goods on arrival.

Deliver

Capture receiver, signature and POD — close the loop.

Reconcile

Bills, receipts, cash book and P&L update themselves.

Connected by design

Your branches,
finally in sync.

When Ludhiana dispatches a challan, Tiruppur knows. When a delivery is completed, the bill is ready. When a payment comes in, outstanding drops on its own.

  • Destination branch alerted on every dispatch
  • Transfer challans with in-transit / arrived status
  • Short and damaged goods recorded at unloading
  • Money posted to cash, bank and ledgers automatically
