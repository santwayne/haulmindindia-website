General

What is HaulMind India?

HaulMind India is a transport management platform for parcel and transport businesses. It connects LR booking, dispatch, branch transfers, unloading, delivery, billing and accounts in one system.

Who is it built for?

Parcel services, transport companies, goods carriers and fleet operators with one or many branches — from the owner to branch managers, booking clerks and accounts staff.

Can I manage multiple branches?

Yes. HaulMind is built for multi-branch operations: hubs and branches are linked, owners see everything, and staff only see their own branch.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. HaulMind runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, so branch staff and delivery teams can use it anywhere.

Features

Does it support billing and accounts?

Yes. Party billing, receipts (cash, UPI, bank, cheque), ledgers, outstanding ageing, cash and bank books, expenses, payables and profit & loss reports are all included.

Can customers track their LR?

Yes. There is a public LR tracking page where anyone can check shipment status with the LR number — try it on our Track LR page.

Are parties informed automatically?

Yes. Booked, arrived and delivered messages can go out on WhatsApp or SMS, and every message is logged.

Can it calculate freight automatically?

Yes. Freight is calculated from party and route rates, and HaulMind warns you when a party crosses its credit limit.

Setup & security

Can I bring my old data in?

Yes. Parties with opening balances, brokers, past LRs and cash / bank openings can be imported from Excel or CSV (including Vouch exports), with a preview before saving.

Is my data safe?

Every employee gets their own login with one of six roles, access is limited by branch, there is a full audit log and an automatic daily backup.

Will the LR and bilty carry my company name?

Yes. Your company name is printed on LRs, challans, delivery challans and messages.
