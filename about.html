HaulMind India is built by Wayne E Solutions — a software and digital team from Ludhiana building products for businesses in India and Canada.

Our story

Built from real
transport problems.

HaulMind started as AI-powered trucking software for fleets in Canada. HaulMind India takes the same foundation and rebuilds it around how Indian parcel and transport businesses actually work — LRs, bilty, nag, challans, to-pay and TBB, hubs and branches.

Every module was shaped by real requirement lists from transport operators: what the booking clerk needs at the counter, what the branch manager checks every morning, and what the owner wants to see at the end of the month.
