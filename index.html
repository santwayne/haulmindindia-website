GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

What is HaulMind India? ＋

HaulMind India is a transport management platform for parcel and transport businesses. It connects LR booking, dispatch, branch transfers, delivery, billing and accounts.

Can I manage multiple branches? ＋

Yes. HaulMind is designed for multi-branch operations, with central visibility for owners and branch-scoped access for staff.

Does it support billing and accounts? ＋

Yes. The platform includes party billing, receipts, ledgers, outstanding ageing, cash and bank books, expenses, payables and profit & loss reports.

Can customers track their LR? ＋

HaulMind includes a public LR tracking page so shipment status can be checked using the LR number.
